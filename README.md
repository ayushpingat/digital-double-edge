# 📱 Digital Double-Edge

### Smart Digital Wellness Platform for Engineering Students

**Digital Double-Edge** is a smart digital wellness platform designed to analyze smartphone usage habits among engineering students and understand how excessive or unbalanced smartphone usage can affect **academic performance, productivity, sleep, and mental well-being**.

The project aims to help students become more aware of their digital habits and encourage healthier, more productive technology usage.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Smartphones have become an essential part of students' daily lives. While they provide access to education, communication, entertainment, and productivity tools, excessive usage can negatively impact important aspects of student life.

Engineering students often spend significant amounts of time on smartphones for both academic and non-academic activities.

This project focuses on understanding:

- 📱 Smartphone usage patterns
- 📚 Impact on academic performance
- ⏱️ Effect on productivity
- 😴 Relationship with sleep habits
- 🧠 Possible effects on mental well-being
- 🎯 Digital habits that may contribute to reduced productivity

---

## 💡 Objectives

The main objectives of Digital Double-Edge are:

1. Analyze smartphone usage habits of engineering students.
2. Identify patterns in daily screen-time and app usage.
3. Study the relationship between smartphone usage and academic performance.
4. Analyze how smartphone usage affects productivity and sleep.
5. Provide meaningful insights through an easy-to-understand interface.
6. Promote healthier and more balanced digital habits.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📊 **Usage Analysis**  
  Analyze smartphone usage patterns and screen-time behavior.

- 📚 **Academic Impact Analysis**  
  Study the relationship between digital habits and academic performance.

- ⏱️ **Productivity Insights**  
  Identify usage patterns that may affect productivity.

- 😴 **Sleep Analysis**  
  Explore the relationship between smartphone usage and sleep habits.

- 🧠 **Wellness Insights**  
  Provide insights into digital habits and overall well-being.

- 📈 **Interactive Dashboard**  
  Present collected data and insights in a simple and understandable format.

- 🎯 **Digital Wellness Awareness**  
  Help students understand their smartphone habits and make informed changes.
